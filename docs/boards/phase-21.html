5 · Removing someone, with the limit said plainly; and stopping

Remove Rob?

Rob can no longer open this page or see new changes. You will get a new key for the page and everyone else switches to it.

Rob keeps anything he already saw or copied. Removing someone cannot take that back.

Finishing…

Rob is removed. New edits stay sealed under the old key until this device switches everyone to the new one. Keep Seijaku open for a moment.

If this device goes offline, it finishes next time it connects; the panel says “Finishing…” until then.

Stop sharing with people?

The shared page goes away for everyone. Each person keeps a read-only copy of what they last had, marked “No longer shared”.

Decisions taken (from the design doc)

Invite by link only, one use, 7 days. Only the owner invites and removes. History of a shared page: owner only. One extra WebSocket per shared page that is open. Server-enforced read-only (a reader holds the page key, so this is the server's rule, not cryptography). Removal rotates the key and cannot undo what someone already saw. Left out: comments, live cursors, sub-pages, editors inviting others, groups.
